Medicine Label Simplifier 💊

Ever tried reading a prescription or OTC medicine box and felt lost in the wall of fine print, complex dosages, and medical jargon? You’re not alone.

Medicine Label Simplifier is an AI-powered web app designed to bridge the gap between technical pharmaceutical packaging and everyday understanding. Simply upload a photo of a medicine label, and the app breaks down complex packaging instructions into a clear, structured summary you can actually read and use.

💡 Features
📷 Smart Image-to-Text Processing: Upload any clear photo of a medicine label to extract text instantly using OCR.

🧠 Context-Aware AI Summaries: Combines Natural Language Processing (NLP) with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to cross-reference trusted medical knowledge bases and explain terms in plain English.

🛠️ How It Works
Scan: The app uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to pull text directly from the packaging image.

Retrieve: A RAG pipeline fetches verified reference context from trusted medical documentation.

Simplify: An LLM synthesizes the extracted text and reference material into a safe, digestible summary.

Save: The structured output is stored in your secure account history for quick future reference.

⚠️ Important Medical Disclaimer
This project is strictly an informational tool.

Medicine Label Simplifier is designed solely to clarify and format text already printed on medicine packaging or available in reference materials. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations, nor does it prescribe medication or alter official dosage instructions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or pharmacist regarding your health and medications.
